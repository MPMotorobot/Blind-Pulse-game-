An atmospheric 2D survival game with stealth elements, where the player controls a small spark of light in a world consumed by darkness.

**Movement reveals the path. Stillness reveals the danger.**

## 🌑 About the Game

Long ago, the Lighthouse of Silence guided ships safely home through the darkest storms. But one day, an ancient darkness known as the Abyss emerged from beneath the sea, releasing a thick fog that extinguished the lighthouse.

Its lens shattered, scattering fragments across the Abyss.

The lighthouse keeper disappeared into the darkness, leaving behind one final spark of light.

You are that spark.

Your mission is to collect the scattered fragments, restore the lighthouse, and guide the lost ships home.

But the fog hides more than fragments. Shadows lurk within it, and the Hounds of the Abyss follow every sound.

## 🎮 Gameplay

Blind Pulse is built around a unique mechanic that forces you to balance movement and stillness.

- **Move to discover.** Moving stirs the fog and makes the scattered light fragments reveal themselves.
- **Stand still to observe.** As the fog settles, hidden Shadows become easier to see.
- **Avoid the darkness.** Shadows roam the environment, while the Hounds of the Abyss can track you when you stop moving.
- **Collect light fragments.** Gather the pieces needed to restore the lighthouse lens.
- **Survive.** Avoid enemies, manage your health, and use power-ups to stay alive.

Every movement is a choice. Every pause can be dangerous.

## ✨ Features

- 15 handcrafted progression stages with different objectives.
- Atmospheric darkness, animated fog, and dynamic lighting effects.
- Two types of hostile entities: Shadows and Hounds of the Abyss.
- Multiple objectives, including collecting fragments, surviving the night, maintaining silence, and lighting beacons.
- Three starting hearts, with collectible shields, extra lives, and healing.

## 🗺️ The Journey

The game takes you through 15 stages:

1. **The Shore** — Collect the first fragments.
2. **The Misty Harbor** — Evade the creatures drawn to the fog.
3. **Night Watch** — Survive until dawn.
4. **The Echoing Gorge** — Navigate a more dangerous darkness.
5. **The Quiet Cove** — Find safety in stillness.
6. **Beacon Lights** — Ignite the scattered beacons.
7. **The Noisy Forest** — Awaken the dormant light through movement.
8. **The Ice Floe** — Search for fragments beneath the cracking ice.
9. **The Long Night** — Endure a prolonged night.
10. **The Ship Graveyard** — Light the beacons of sunken vessels.
11. **The Singing Cliffs** — Move through the ringing rocks.
12. **The Dead Calm** — Find stillness while danger waits.
13. **The Black Tide** — Gather fragments amid the advancing darkness.
14. **The Last Dawn** — Survive the final night.
15. **The Heart of the Abyss** — Collect the final fragments and restore the lighthouse.

## 🕹️ Controls

The game uses pointer-based movement.

- **Move the mouse:** Guide the spark toward the cursor.
- **Stop moving:** Allow the fog to settle and reveal nearby threats.
- **Click:** Start the game, advance through the introduction, continue to the next stage, or retry after defeat.

There is no conventional combat system. Survival depends on movement, observation, and timing.

## ❤️ Survival System

You begin with three hearts.

Colliding with a Shadow costs one heart. Temporary invulnerability after damage provides a brief opportunity to escape.

During your journey, you may discover:

- **Light Shield** — Protects you from the next damaging collision.
- **Extra Heart** — Increases your maximum health and restores one heart.
- **Healing Light** — Restores your health to its maximum.

Lose all your hearts, and the darkness closes in. But every defeat offers another chance to continue.

## 🌌 Atmosphere

Blind Pulse uses a minimalist visual style built around contrast:

- Deep black and midnight-blue environments.
- Dense, animated fog.
- A small golden-white spark representing the player.
- Crimson enemies emerging from the darkness.
- Turquoise light fragments glowing through the mist.
- Subtle particles, gradients, and atmospheric effects.

The experience focuses on tension and uncertainty rather than direct combat.

## 🏁 The Goal

Collect every fragment of the shattered lens, overcome the dangers of the Abyss, and reignite the Lighthouse of Silence.

The journey may take several attempts. The important thing is that the light keeps burning.

**The darkness can make you fall. It cannot decide whether you rise again.**
